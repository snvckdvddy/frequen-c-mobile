## App Store Description (4000 chars max)

Frequen-C is a collaborative music listening app that lets you and your friends experience music together in real time — no matter where you are.

Create a listening room, invite friends with a link or QR code, and take turns choosing the soundtrack. Three room modes let you control the vibe:

**Campfire** — Everyone takes turns. Round-robin queue rotation so each person gets to play their pick.

**Spotlight** — The host curates. Guests suggest tracks and the host approves what makes the cut.

**Open Floor** — Democracy rules. Participants vote to reorder the queue, and the crowd decides what plays next.

Every room comes with live chat, quick reactions, and a shared queue you can see updating in real time. Build your reputation through Crowd Voltage (CV) — earn points by making great picks that people love, or stake your prediction skills in Frequency Forecast mini-games.

Features:
• Real-time synchronized listening rooms
• Three room modes: Campfire, Spotlight, Open Floor
• QR code and deep link room invites
• In-session chat with quick emoji reactions
• Crossfader Duel — head-to-head track battles decided by vote
• Frequency Forecast — predict the next hit and earn CV
• Track search across music catalogs
• Drag-to-reorder queue management
• Session history and favorite tracks
• Push notifications for room activity
• Dark mode interface built for nighttime listening

Frequen-C is designed for the moments when music is better shared — late-night study sessions, road trips, parties, or just hanging with friends across different cities.

## App Review Notes
Frequen-C requires an internet connection and at least two users to demonstrate the collaborative listening features. For review purposes, you can:
1. Create an account with any email/username
2. Create a listening room in any mode
3. Search for tracks and add them to the queue
4. Open a second simulator/device to join the room via the join code

The app uses mock/demo mode for music playback — actual streaming integration with music services is planned for a future update. Current version demonstrates the social listening coordination features.
