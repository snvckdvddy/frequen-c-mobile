Frequen-C Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

Frequen-C ("we," "our," or "the app") is a collaborative music listening application developed by Caleb Ruble. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your chosen username and email address. We do not require your real name.

Session Data. When you create or join a listening room, we collect the room name, tracks added, votes, chat messages, and participation timestamps. This data is used to power the shared listening experience.

Device Information. We may collect device type, operating system version, and push notification tokens to deliver notifications and ensure app compatibility.

Usage Analytics. We may collect anonymized usage data such as screen views and feature interactions to improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your location data.
  • We do not access your contacts or phone number.
  • We do not access your music library or listening history outside of Frequen-C sessions.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the collaborative listening experience.
  • To send push notifications about session activity (e.g., when someone joins your room or a track changes).
  • To display your username to other participants in a session.
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs.

4. Third-Party Services

Frequen-C may integrate with third-party music services (such as Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, or Tidal) for track search and playback. When you connect a music service, that service's own privacy policy applies to data shared with them. We only access the minimum information needed to search for and play tracks.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your account credentials are stored securely using encrypted device storage. Session data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/WSS). We retain session data only for the duration of the session plus a reasonable period for history features.

6. Camera Access

Frequen-C requests camera access solely to scan QR codes for joining listening rooms. Camera data is processed on-device and is never stored or transmitted.

7. Push Notifications

You can enable or disable push notifications at any time through your device settings. We use notifications to alert you about session events like new participants and track changes.

8. Children's Privacy

Frequen-C is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your account information in the Profile screen.
  • Deletion: You can request account deletion by contacting us. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.
  • Opt-Out: You can disable notifications and disconnect music services at any time from the app's settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by updating the effective date above.
